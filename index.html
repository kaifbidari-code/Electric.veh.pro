Safety Engineering

Mandatory Seven-in-One Protection System

Every protective subsystem is physically built into the 3D model, integrated into the reactive state machine, and testable via live simulation controls below.

FEATURE 01 CONNECTED

Automatic Cable Retraction

Integrated motorized reel with continuous tension feedback. Eliminates ground tripping hazards, water ponding contact, and cable wear by automatically rewinding the 5-meter heavy-duty cable after disconnect.

Rotating 3D reel with Catmull-Rom spline tension curve.
FEATURE 02 SECURE

Cable Theft & Tampering Detection

Continuous electrical continuity pilot loop coupled with strain-gauge sensors inside the locking collar. Any unauthorized cut or forced pull triggers an instant audible siren, flashes hazard red beacon, and trips isolation relays.

Activates flashing red LED, alarm siren and remote alert.
FEATURE 03 NORMAL

Internal Temperature Monitoring

Precision thermistors monitor internal power contactors and cable housing. Normal (<45°C), Warning (45-65°C: auxiliary cooling fan), and Critical (>65°C: immediate safe charging cutoff and remote alarm).

FEATURE 04 NO FAULTS

Automatic 6-Fault Electrical Detection

Comprehensive hardware trip monitoring for Overcurrent, Overvoltage, Undervoltage, Short Circuit, Earth Leakage (RCD 30mA), and Abnormal Temperature with physical contactor isolation.

Dual-layer silicone rubber gaskets, sloped rain-deflector canopy, sealed touchscreen bezel, and protected downward cable entry designed to achieve IP65 ingress protection against heavy monsoon rain and dust.

About The Project

The SmartGuard EV Vision

Electric two-wheelers represent the fastest-growing segment of global zero-emission urban transportation. However, outdoor charging infrastructure currently suffers from dangling cables that become tripping hazards, rain exposure risks, cable theft/vandalism, and lack of real-time thermal safety monitoring.

SmartGuard EV addresses these critical challenges through a holistic 7-in-1 industrial design. By pairing automated cable retraction with continuous thermal surveillance, anti-theft sensors, IP65 weatherproofing, and safe electrical isolation, SmartGuard EV delivers a robust, secure, and user-friendly charging experience for next-generation electric mobility.
