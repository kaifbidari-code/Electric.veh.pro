INDUSTRY CHALLENGES

The Outdoor EV Charging Dilemma

Critical pain points facing unattended electric two-wheeler charging infrastructure.

⚠️ Cable Clutter & Tripping Hazards

Unmanaged cables lying on parking surfaces create serious pedestrian hazards and suffer continuous vehicle tyre crushing damage.

🌧️ Monsoon Rain & Water Exposure

Outdoor chargers face harsh water jets and dust ingress. Unsealed cables in standing water cause catastrophic insulation breakdowns.

🔒 Cable Theft & Copper Vandalism

Public chargers regularly suffer from severed cables and stolen copper lines without real-time tamper alarms or physical security latches.

🔥 Contactor Thermal Buildup

Continuous 16A current flow leads to internal thermal rise. Without pre-emptive cutoff, stations risk thermal runaway and equipment damage.

SAFETY ENGINEERING

7-in-1 Protection System (Part 1)

Physical 3D implementation & simulated interactive responses.

FEATURE 01

Cable Retraction

Motorized BLDC reel with constant tension governor. Automatically rewinds cable into housing, preventing trips and tyre crush damage.

FEATURE 02

Theft & Tamper Alarm

Continuity pilot loop and strain sensor. Unauthorized pull or cut activates hazard red beacon, audible siren, and trips safe isolation relays.

FEATURE 03

Thermal Monitoring

Dual NTC thermistors near contactors. Normal (<45°C), Warning (45-65°C), and Critical (>65°C) with automatic charging shutdown.
